Problem: Divide Array Into Two Sum Groups

Logic:
1. Initialize two variables:
   - `num1 = 0` to store the sum of numbers that are NOT divisible by `m`.
   - `num2 = 0` to store the sum of numbers that ARE divisible by `m`.

2. Traverse all integers from `1` to `n` (inclusive).

3. For each number:
   - If the number is divisible by `m` (`i % m == 0`), add it to `num2`.
   - Otherwise, add it to `num1`.

4. After traversing all numbers, compute the required result:
      num1 - num2

5. Return the result.

Why it works:
- Every integer in the range [1, n] belongs to exactly one of two groups:
  - Divisible by `m`
  - Not divisible by `m`
- By maintaining separate sums for these two groups, we can directly compute the required difference (`num1 - num2`) at the end.

Time Complexity: O(n)
- Each integer from 1 to n is processed exactly once.

Space Complexity: O(1)
- Only two integer variables are used to store the sums.
