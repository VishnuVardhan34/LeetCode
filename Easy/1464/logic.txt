Approach:
1. Sort the array in ascending order.
2. The two largest elements will be at the last two indices of the sorted array.
3. Since the expression is (nums[i] - 1) * (nums[j] - 1), the maximum value is obtained by choosing the two largest numbers.
4. Compute:
      (largest - 1) * (second_largest - 1)
5. Return the computed value.

Example:
nums = [3, 4, 5, 2]

After sorting:
[2, 3, 4, 5]

Largest elements:
5 and 4

Result:
(5 - 1) * (4 - 1)
= 4 * 3
= 12

Time Complexity:
- Sorting the array: O(n log n)
- Computing the answer: O(1)
Overall: O(n log n)

Space Complexity:
- O(1) (ignoring the space used internally by the sorting algorithm)
